EXPLORING THE COMPLEXITIES OF HUMAN DESIGN THROUGH EMOTIONAL EXPRESSION AND HISTORICAL PRACTICES.

I work with oil, ink and charcoal, experiment with egg tempera, fresco and silverpoint. I source most of my materials, making my own paints, mediums, brushes and panels. Using many experimental methods I express the emotional aspects of humanity using famous literary works and personal experiences.
